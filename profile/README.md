## How we work

- **Workflow first.** Understand the work before adding AI to it.
- **Governance included.** Data boundaries, human review, and audit trails are part of the build, not an afterthought.
- **Content is the foundation.** Deep ECM and document-workflow experience for content-heavy operations.
- **Practical automation, not AI theater.** Every engagement has a measurable business case — and we will say "don't automate this yet" when the numbers don't support a build.
- **Hybrid by design.** Cloud where appropriate, local and private AI where control matters, human review where accountability matters.

One size fits one: solutions built around how each business actually works, delivered through a standardized operations method.
